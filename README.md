## 📊 The Position Ladder

*Scale in one level at a time. Don't skip your stops.*

| Level | Position | Focus | Chapters |
|:-----:|----------|-------|:--------:|
| 🟢 **L1** | `BEGINNER` | Foundation — how the market physically works | `1–5` |
| 🔵 **L2** | `INTERMEDIATE` | Analysis, styles & discipline | `6–12` |
| 🟠 **L3** | `ADVANCED` | Derivatives, macro & portfolio | `13–19` |
