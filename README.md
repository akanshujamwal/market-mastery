# 🛡️ Risk Comes Before Returns

The core principle of this roadmap is:

> **Survival before returns.**

A strategy is not useful simply because it can generate profits in a favorable period.

A complete trading framework should answer:

```text
How much can I lose?
        ↓
How much capital is at risk?
        ↓
What happens during a losing streak?
        ↓
What happens during high volatility?
        ↓
What happens when the strategy stops working?
```

Think in terms of **risk units (R)** rather than only rupee profits.

For example:

```text
1R = predefined amount of risk

+2R  → gain equal to 2× planned risk
-1R  → loss equal to planned risk
-3R  → loss equal to 3× planned risk
```

The exact value of `R` should be determined by your own risk framework and financial circumstances.

# 📝 The Trading Journal

Journaling is one of the most important practical components of the roadmap.

For every trade, record:

* Date and time
* Instrument
* Setup
* Market context
* Entry
* Stop
* Target
* Position size
* Risk in ₹
* Risk in R
* Exit
* P&L
* Screenshot
* Reason for entry
* Reason for exit
* Mistakes
* Emotional state
* Lesson learned

### Weekly Review

Ask:

1. Which setups performed well?
2. Which setups consistently failed?
3. Did I follow my rules?
4. Where did I take unnecessary risk?
5. Did I overtrade?
6. What should change next week?

**Your journal turns trading activity into data.**

# 🧪 Backtesting & Validation

Before trusting a strategy, understand the difference between:

```text
Idea
 ↓
Rules
 ↓
Historical Backtest
 ↓
Out-of-Sample Test
 ↓
Forward Test
 ↓
Small-Scale Deployment
 ↓
Monitoring
```

Evaluate more than just total returns.

Consider:

* Win rate
* Average win
* Average loss
* Maximum drawdown
* Profit factor
* Expectancy
* Sharpe ratio
* Sortino ratio
* Number of trades
* Losing streaks
* Transaction costs
* Slippage

A strategy that looks excellent before costs and poor after costs teaches an important lesson:

> **Execution is part of the strategy.**

# 🎯 The End Goal

The goal of this roadmap is **not** to turn you into someone who predicts every market move.

That is not a realistic objective.

The goal is to develop a process that helps you:

```text
Understand the market
        ↓
Form a hypothesis
        ↓
Define the risk
        ↓
Execute according to rules
        ↓
Record the outcome
        ↓
Analyze the data
        ↓
Improve the process
```

The market will always contain uncertainty.

Your job is not to eliminate uncertainty.

**Your job is to manage it.**
